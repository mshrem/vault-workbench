ORIGINAL FILES

One collection. Kept local.

Checking your library connection…

The app automatically looks for Desktop\USSA VAULT on Windows. For another location, start it with:

.\start-windows.cmd --library "D:\USSA VAULT"

On Unraid, mount the folder containing VAULT and PEGBOARD at /library, read-only. See README.md in the app folder.

Preview models are generated locally from your connected STL library. Filament & colors totals the sliced weights you enter. Print time requires your slicer.

LOCAL LIBRARY IMPORTER

Bring the rest of your collection into view.

Build lightweight previews and measure your connected STLs on this computer. Start with Drawers; other categories can follow. You can stop and resume without redoing finished files. Choose how many files to convert at once.

One-time Windows setup: double-click setup-importer-windows.cmd in the extracted app folder. Then close the running console with Ctrl+C and launch start-windows.cmd again. Setup needs internet; importing stays local.

Larger binary STLs still get dimensions. A higher limit retries their previews and may use several GB of RAM per worker. Start with 2 workers; use 1 if your server is busy. Sources too complex for a lightweight preview remain available for download.

Diagnostics stay local in appdata and keep roughly 3 MB of rotating log files. A support bundle contains relative part filenames, dependency versions and recent events; review it before sharing. Nothing is sent automatically. Previews appear in Parts library. Imported S/M/L drawer meshes are available in the configurator. Parts that only have dimensions can be exported, but need a cached mesh for assembly preview. XL, split-width modules and sustainers remain available for individual inspection.
